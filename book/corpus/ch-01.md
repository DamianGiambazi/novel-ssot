A kitchen. Early morning. Late autumn. The light is grey and thin. The protagonist is making coffee. The routine is precise, ritualistic. They have done this ten thousand times.

While waiting for the kettle, the protagonist looks out the window and sees a neighbour's house with a single light on in an upstairs room. A figure is standing in the window. The protagonist watches for a moment. The figure doesn't move. Then the light goes off.

The protagonist thinks, briefly, "I wonder if they ever leave that room."

The protagonist turns away from the window. The kettle clicks. They pour the water. They do not look back.
