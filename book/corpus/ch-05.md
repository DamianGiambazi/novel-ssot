The kitchen. Wednesday, 7:25. Two days before the deadline. Sarah's second morning.

Sarah arrives early. She goes to the dresser for her tea. The middle drawer does not open. She tries it again. She looks at the protagonist.

The protagonist makes a joke. It fails. Sarah understands the drawer is locked. It was not locked yesterday. Her tea is inside it.

The protagonist changes the subject badly: the 9:00 quarterly review they are presenting. They check the time. They check their phone.

Sarah: "You don't have to keep pretending this is what you wanted. I'm not asking you to fix it. I'm asking you to admit it."

The protagonist: "I'm fine. I chose this."

Sarah leaves on foot. She does not wait for the lift. The protagonist misses the 9:00 review — the first absence in eleven years. They are standing in the kitchen at 9:00.

Late that day Sarah's car is fixed. She texts: "Car's back. Thanks for the lifts." The tea is still in the drawer.
