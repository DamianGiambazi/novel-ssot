The kitchen at night. Late winter. The dresser.

They unlock the drawer and read the whole letter for the first time. Two pages. There is nothing in it. No accusation, no demand, no confession required. Daniel writes about the weather in Lisbon, his work, a dog that died. The last line: "Nothing is owed. I just didn't want to be a secret any more."

The threat was never in the envelope. It was the act of not opening it.

The protagonist does not go to the window.

They put the letter back in the drawer and leave the drawer unlocked.
