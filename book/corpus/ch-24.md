A kitchen. Early morning. Late autumn, one year on. The light is grey and thin. The house is half in boxes.

The kettle is on. The protagonist looks out of the window. The light is on across the street. The figure is standing in the window. The protagonist watches for a moment. The figure doesn't move.

The protagonist turns away first. The kettle clicks. They do not pour the water. They pick up their coat — the extra layer — and go out by the door.

At the gate they look back. Their own kitchen window is empty. Across the street, the light is still on.
